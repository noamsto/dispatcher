usage: crew retro [--report [--json]]
       crew retro --draft [--crew ID] [--all]

Read-only rollup of the retro notes workers and the dispatcher post to the
synthetic retro: and metrics: sinks.

  (no flags)  One row per run: branch, engine, model, tier, outcome, tags
  --report    Aggregate rollup of tags across runs
  --json      Machine-readable; only with --report
  --draft     Print one crew's session_summary note, ready to post: one segment
              per worker — codename, {tier,engine,model}, outcome, tags — plus
              any tag two or more of them carried
  --crew      Crew for --draft; default this checkout's, else $CREW_ID
  --all       --draft: every branch of the crew, not only the ones dispatched
              after its newest session_summary note

The rollup takes no crew filter and no positional: notes are cross-run evidence,
like `rate`. --draft reads the one crew it summarises, and takes neither
--report nor --json.

  crew retro --report
  crew retro --draft --all
