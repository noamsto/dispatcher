usage: crew peer-claims <issue>

Print the live claim a peer host holds on issue (digits only) as one line, as
dispatch's claim gate reads it: a fresh dispatch row on feat/<issue>-*, or a
fresh claim-issue row, with no later reap of its branch. Prints nothing and
exits 0 when no peer holds one; exits 1 when a peer store file is unreadable, and
2 for a missing or non-numeric issue.

  crew peer-claims 42
