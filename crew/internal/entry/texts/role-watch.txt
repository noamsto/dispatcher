usage: crew role-watch <role> --pane <id> [--branch B] [--engine E]
                       [--interval S] [--defer-notice S] [--submit-retries N]

Per-role-pane supervisor: reads the crew bus, admits only the role's lead or
dispatcher, queues their assignments and types each into the role pane only when
a capture shows an idle input box, then verifies the submit. Ends when the pane
is gone, marked exited, or no longer stamped with this role, branch and crew
(queued work is reported as orphaned).

  <role>            Role the pane serves (never lead)
  --pane            tmux pane id to type into, %N (required)
  --branch          Branch the role works on (default: the current branch)
  --engine          Engine family (claude|codex|cursor|pi) whose composer is read
                    (default claude)
  --interval        Poll interval (default 2)
  --defer-notice    Seconds a held assignment waits before the lead is told (default 60)
  --submit-retries  Extra submit attempts before an assignment is reported (default 3)

  crew role-watch reviewer --pane %N --branch feat/x
