usage: crew identity <branch> [crew]
       crew identity --hash <name>
       crew identity --occupied [--crew ID]

Print the codename and colours for <branch>, assigning one from the pool on first
call; --hash goes the other way, from a name to its identity; --occupied prints,
one per line, the codenames already taken here (live dispatches and tmux windows,
mirror windows included), for a remote dispatch to avoid.

  <branch>  Branch whose identity to print or assign
  [crew]    Crew the assignment is recorded for; defaults to this repo's crew
  --crew    Crew id, in place of the [crew] word

  crew identity feat/x
