usage: crew policy set <name> <text>
       crew policy clear <name>
       crew policy show <name> [--json]
       crew policy list [--json | --brief]

A standing policy is the repo owner's own words, kept for an unattended
dispatcher to act on while they are away (skill standing-policy). set and clear
are the owner's alone: they refuse a worker, role, overseer, launcher or
crew-pane session, and any session without a terminal on stdin. show and list
are open to anyone and only report a policy that is in force: a record that is
not the owner's own, not for this repo or not a plain file is refused with its
reason, and list names it on stderr.
A record lives in ${XDG_STATE_HOME:-$HOME/.local/state}/crew/policy/<repo>/,
outside the repo and its bus, one file per name. Set of a name again replaces it.

  <name>   1-32 characters: a-z, 0-9 and -, starting with a letter or digit
  <text>   The policy in your words, one argument; kept verbatim
  --json   show: the record as one JSON object; list: an array of records
  --brief  list: one line per policy in force, for a launcher prompt

  crew policy set night 'merge when green and reviewed; file nothing new'
  crew policy show night
  crew policy list --brief
  crew policy clear night
