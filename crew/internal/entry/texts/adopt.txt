usage: crew adopt [--force | --reattach] <id> [pid]

Re-attach to an on-disk crew after a restart lost CREW_ID.

  --force     Adopt even though another pid is recorded as its dispatcher
  --reattach  Re-register only if no live dispatcher holds the crew (see below)
  <id>        Crew id, from `crew crews`
  [pid]       Dispatcher pid to record; defaults to the nearest non-shell ancestor

--force is stripped from anywhere in the args, so it can never land as the pid.
Adopting a crew whose recorded dispatcher pid is dead also releases the
`dispatched` label from the GitHub issues that crew claimed but never finished.

--reattach requires both <id> and [pid]; a missing one is a usage error. It
re-registers the crew to the first non-shell process at or above [pid] when
that process is an engine (claude, codex, cursor-agent, node, pi) running in
$TMUX_PANE, and no live dispatcher pid is recorded. It never runs from a worker
or role pane, never takes --force, never releases claims, and is silent unless
it writes. dispatch runs it on every local dispatch (not on the remote side or
under DISPATCH_PRECHECK), passing its own $PPID.

  crew adopt <crew-id>
