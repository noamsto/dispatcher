usage: crew decide "<text>"

Record a dispatcher decision on this bus as a decision row (from
dispatcher:<crew>, addressed to nobody). The peer sync carries it to every
peer host, where `crew decisions` lists it with this host as its origin.

Only a dispatcher can decide: a worker or role session is refused. The text
must be non-empty, at most 3500 bytes and fit a bus line once encoded, since a
row a peer would drop must not look shared. A peer receives decisions up to 24
hours old. Exits 1 on a refusal, 2 for missing text.

  crew decide "use sqlite for the cache"
