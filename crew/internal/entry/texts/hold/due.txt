usage: crew hold due [--crew ID] [--json [--with-parked]]

Only the holds that may be dispatched now. A quota hold is due when its
quota window has already reset, a fleet hold when the fleet queue admits it (at
most one machine-wide). Take a due fleet hold with `dispatch --fleet-hold <id>`,
then `crew hold release <id>`: a plain dispatch is refused by the queue's
admission. Fleet holds the queue parked are listed apart and never due. Exits 0
when something is due, 1 when nothing is, 5 when the fleet queue cannot be
evaluated (nothing is printed).

  --crew         Crew id; defaults to this repo's crew
  --json         The due holds, one array of hold rows
  --with-parked  With --json: one object, {"due": [...], "parked": [...]}, both
                 arrays of hold rows, each parked row with an added "reason"

The table lists the due rows, then, if any hold is parked, a block headed
`parked  reason  branch  title`. The reason is demoted (it was the due hold for
fleetHeadTimeout, was demoted, and was the due hold for another without being
released), oversize (heavier than fleetBudget, so the queue never admits it) or
ineligible (its crew has no live dispatcher in any registered repo, or its lane
is dead or stale). A parked hold stays outstanding until `crew hold release
<id>`: for demoted or oversize, release it, then re-add the task as a new fleet
hold (an oversize one at a tier within fleetBudget), or ask the human. An
ineligible hold cannot be fixed by releasing and re-adding it: tell the human.

  crew hold due --json
