usage: crew hold due [--crew ID] [--json]

Only the holds that may be dispatched now. A quota hold is due when its
quota window has already reset, a fleet hold when the fleet queue admits it (at
most one machine-wide). Fleet holds the queue parked, as demoted or oversize,
are listed apart and never due. Exits 0 when something is due, 1 when nothing
is.

  --crew  Crew id; defaults to this repo's crew
  --json  One object, {"due": [...], "parked": [...]}: both arrays of hold rows,
          each parked row with an added "reason"

The table lists the due rows, then, if any hold is parked, a block headed
`parked  reason  branch  title`.

  crew hold due --json
