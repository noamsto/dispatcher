usage: crew crews [--mine | --home]

List every crew with traffic on this repo's bus: id, last and first event, worker
count, dispatcher pid and whether that pid is alive. Both sources count — a crew
dir alone (watch creates one) and an id seen only in the log. Layout-2 crews
homed in this repo are listed too; run outside a repo, it lists the layout-2
crews with no home repo.

  --mine  Print only the crews this caller owns — one id per line, no header —
          meaning the crews whose recorded dispatcher pid is a live ancestor of
          this process: the one it registered plus any 'crew adopt' re-attached
          to it. A dispatcher that adopted a restarted crew's workers uses this
          to watch every crew at once.

  --home  List only the crews homed in this repo, not ones that merely dispatched
          workers into it from another repo. Exclusive with --mine. A crew is home
          by its homes/<id> record (written by register and adopt); a crew with
          no record, or one naming another repo, counts when its pidfile.log has
          a register or adopt ok/forced line, or its live dispatcher pid runs in
          this repo.

  crew crews
  crew crews --mine
  crew crews --home
