usage: crew register [pid]

Register this crew on the repo bus. N crews may share a repo, each keyed by its
crew id, so there is no cross-crew lock.

CREW_LAYOUT=2 creates the crew's own store under
${XDG_STATE_HOME:-~/.local/state}/crew/crews/<id>/ with a meta.json, and works
outside a repo. An id with an existing crew on this repo's bus stays layout 1,
and an existing meta.json is reused whatever CREW_LAYOUT says.

  [pid]  Long-lived dispatcher pid to record; defaults to the nearest non-shell
         ancestor, which is what an agent's shell tool needs

  crew register
