## Interfaces fixed up front

```
permission-check --pane <%id> --branch <b> --worktree <dir> [--crew-dir <dir>] [--answer]
permission-check --capture <file> --branch <b> --worktree <dir> [--crew-dir <dir>]
                 [--projects-dir <dir>] [--ro-root <dir>]...
```

- stdout is exactly one line: `allow-once` (exit 0) or `human: <reason>`
  (exit 1). Usage errors: `human: usage: …`, exit 2. Nothing else on stdout.
- `--crew-dir` default: `$(git rev-parse --path-format=absolute --git-common-dir)/crew`.
- `--projects-dir` and `--ro-root` are refused (exit 2) outside `--capture`.
  With `--pane`, immutable roots are the build-substituted
  `@protocolDir@ @skillsDir@ @reviewersDir@ @criticsDir@`; a value still
  starting with `@` (unsubstituted source run) contributes no root. A root is
  immutable only if its `realpath` starts with `/nix/store/`, or it came from
  `--ro-root` in `--capture` mode (tests).
- Settle: `--pane` sleeps `max(10, PERMISSION_CHECK_SETTLE)` between the two
  observations; `--capture` sleeps `PERMISSION_CHECK_SETTLE` (default 0).
- Internal functions (one concern each): `frame_parse` (sets `F_AGENT`,
  `F_BLOCK1`, `F_BLOCK2`, `F_NBLOCK`), `transcript_pending` (prints the single
  pending call as compact JSON `{id,file,agent,command,description}` or fails
  with a reason), `lex` (fills arrays `W` words and `WQ` had-quote flags,
  separators as the reserved words `&&`, `;`, `|`), `grammar_check`,
  `path_check <path> <kind>` with kind `file|dir|file-or-dir|walk-root`,
  `refuse <reason>` (prints `human: <reason>`, exits 1).
